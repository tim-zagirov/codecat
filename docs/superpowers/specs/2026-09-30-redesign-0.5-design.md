## 1. Why

0.4 and the island-live-state branch taught the island to *know* a lot — a dot
per session, the agent's step, what a turn handed back. The design did not keep
up:

- **The signal that matters most is the weakest.** An agent waiting for
  permission is a pulsing dot and a cat that — in the default skin — lowers its
  head and opens its mouth. People read that pose as the cat being sick.
- **The glow is a stain.** A radial wash behind the sprite reads as a smudge,
  not as the island's state.
- **Two menus, one of them a settings dump.** Hover shows sessions; click shows
  sessions plus two disclosure groups of skins and switches. The island is for
  sessions; switches belong in a window.
- **Things the product knows are still invisible**: whether the Mac is being
  kept awake, *what* an agent is asking before you open the terminal.
- **The brand is borrowed.** The mascot is a third-party sprite and the icon is a
  generic cartoon cat.

The redesign follows the best notch apps and Apple's Dynamic Island rules
(research in the session of 2026-09-29): compact is one piece of information
split across the notch; the island is one object that grows on a spring;
attention is a short self-opening *peek* with the cause, never a system
notification on top.

## 2. Scope

In, in shipping order (§15 turns each into a milestone):

1. **Poses** — a better frame for each meaning in the LuizMelo pack.
2. **Rim light** — the state lights the island's rim (edge flow) instead of a
   glow behind the sprite. New compact right wing.
3. **Hover inhale** — the island acknowledges the cursor before it opens.
4. **One-level expanded island** — cards, idle fold, power footer, empty and
   first-run states, width and height on one spring, content blurs in.
5. **Peek** — the island opens itself for waiting, crashed and done, with the
   reason; replaces the "while you were away" section.
6. **Settings window** — every switch leaves the island and the panel.
7. **Floating cat on a capsule** — the floating mode speaks the island's
   language.
8. **Icon and menu-bar symbol** — an original mark.

Out (decided):

- **Right-click on the island and click-to-open.** The closed island is
  click-through on purpose (S15, `HoverHostingView.hitTest`): its wings overlap
  the app menu and other apps' status items. The Figma frames that mention a
  right-click or "click skips the dwell" are corrected; Settings opens from
  "•••" in the island, from the status-bar menu and with ⌘, while CodeCat's
  window is key.
- **"Open on hover" switch.** With click-through kept, hover is the only way the
  island opens; a switch that turns it off would leave no way in. The hover
  delay stays adjustable.
- **Choosing a sound.** One switch, the existing sounds (`Purr`, `Glass`).
- **Keyboard navigation in the island.** It must not take focus from the
  terminal being typed in.
- **Hiding the peek when you are already looking at that terminal tab.**
  Detecting the focused tab reliably is expensive; the gain is small.
- **The site case study.** Rewritten after 0.5.0 ships, from real renders.

## 3. Poses (`CodeCatCore/MascotSkins.swift`)

LuizMelo, all six cats (every one has `Walk` ×8, `Sitting` ×1, `Run` ×8 —
checked):

| State | 0.4 | 0.5 | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| sleeping | Sleeping1/2 @ 0.6 fps | unchanged | reads right |
| working | `Idle` ×10 @ 8 | **`Walk` ×8 @ 8 fps** | standing still said "waiting"; walking says "busy" |
| waiting | `Meow` ×4 @ 5 | **`Sitting` ×1** (still) | Meow read as being sick; a cat sitting and looking at you is "I'm waiting". Motion comes from the rim lap and the dot |
| done | Stretching ×2 → Laying → sleep | unchanged | loops well |
| problem | `Itch` ×2 @ 3 (Cat-3: `Licking 2`) | **`Run` frames 6–7 @ 2 fps** for all six | a crouched cat reads as startled; the red does the rest. Cat-3's special case goes away |

Elthen and mxmaze keep their mappings (their waiting frames raise a paw; no one
reads those as illness).

The island's sprite scale is recomputed from the union bounding box of the
frames each skin uses (`SpriteScale`), so the change of frames can change the
scale. Measured with PIL over Cat-1's sheets: the union of every frame is
30×16 px, which lands on ×2 in the island (60×32 ≤ the 60 pt cap) and ×4
floating (120×64 ≤ 120). Tests must assert these numbers for all six cats,
computed the same way, not assume them.

## 4. Compact island (`IslandView`)

Geometry is unchanged: 32 pt tall, 72 pt wings, the measured notch, 10 pt
concave fillets, 16 pt bottom radius.

### 4.1 Rim light — edge flow (replaces the tone glow)

Delete the radial glow behind the cat. The state is drawn on the island:

- **Edge stroke.** An open path along both walls and the bottom
  (`IslandLayout.rimPath`, new: from the left wall at `y = edgeRadius`, down,
  around the bottom corners, up the right wall), inset 0.75 pt, 1.5 pt wide,
  round caps. Stroked with a horizontal `LinearGradient` across the island's
  width: tone at 0 % opacity at both ends → 55 % at 10 % / 90 % of the width →
  95 % at `h ± 0.14` → white 100 % at the highlight position `h`. The walls sit
  at the ends of the gradient, so they fade out toward the screen edge by
  construction.
- **Bloom.** A drop shadow of the tone under the island, opacity 0.32, radius
  10, y 2 (compact); 0.40 / 14 / 3 for peek and expanded. Not drawn for
  sleeping.
- **Highlight position `h`.** 0.5 and still for working, done and problem. For
  waiting it travels: `h = 0.1 + 0.8 · frac(t / 2.4 s)`, a lap every 2.4 s,
  computed from a timestamp (`TimelineView(.animation)`), so a view rebuild
  does not restart it. This is the product's only loop; it stops the moment no
  session waits.
- **Sleeping.** No stroke, no bloom.
- **Tone change.** The stroke's colours and the bloom cross-fade over 0.25 s
  ease-out (`Motion.toneCrossfade`).

### 4.2 Right wing

The wing shows live data, never a logo, in the aggregate's type and colour:

| Sessions (non-idle) | Right wing |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | empty (0.4 showed a grey dot; the plate is quiet now) |
| 1, working, with steps | `2/5` (12 pt semibold, tabular, tone) + a 16 pt progress ring (track white 16 %, arc tone, 2.5 pt, round cap, from 12 o'clock) |
| 1, working, no steps | elapsed time `4m` / `1h` in the same style, no ring |
| 1, done | a 16 pt tone disc with a white check (SF Symbol `checkmark`, 9 pt bold) |
| 1, other | its dot |
| 2–4 | one 7 pt dot per session in a single row, spacing 5 (0.4's 2×2 grid goes: a row reads left to right like the list) |
| 5+ | one dot in the aggregate tone + the count |

The waiting dot keeps the existing 3 s pulse and gains a 13 pt halo at 30 %
opacity. A done check lives as long as the session's `done` state (the store's
600 s TTL), then fades with the rim.

### 4.3 Hover inhale

When the cursor enters the silhouette (not the window rectangle), the island
grows 3 pt on each side and 4 pt down on
`Animation.interactiveSpring(response: 0.38, dampingFraction: 0.8)`, the bloom
goes 0.32 → 0.5 / radius 14, a black shadow (0.6, radius 8, y 3) lifts it off
the wallpaper, and the rim's stops are multiplied by 1.3 (clamped). When the
cursor leaves before the dwell ends, it exhales on the same spring.

The closed window is enlarged by the inhale margin so the grown shape is not
clipped; hover and hit-testing use the silhouette path (`IslandLayout.silhouettePath`
at the current size), so the extra window margin never counts as "inside".

## 5. Expanded island — one level

`IslandMenuLevel` (`short`/`full`) is removed. There is one expanded state.

### 5.1 Opening and closing

- Opens after the cursor rests in the silhouette for the **hover delay**
  (setting, default 0.3 s, range 0.15–1.0 s; 0.4's M11 dwell made adjustable).
  A drag in progress never opens it (unchanged).
- Closes 0.15 s after the cursor leaves the expanded silhouette, on Escape, and
  after a row's jump. `.full`'s "stays open until resign-key" behaviour goes
  away with `.full`.
- The window becomes key on the first mouse-down inside it (unchanged
  `OverlayPanel.sendEvent`), so a row works in one click.

### 5.2 Geometry and motion

- Body **420 pt wide**, height = 36 (header band) + list + 12, capped at
  `screen.visibleFrame.height − 32 − 24` with the list scrolling beyond it.
- Fillet 10 → 14 pt and bottom radius 16 → 24 pt, animated with the size.
  `IslandShape` becomes animatable in width, height, fillet and bottom radius
  (`AnimatablePair` of pairs); 0.4 animated only the bottom radius and height.
- **Open:** `spring(response: 0.42, dampingFraction: 0.8)`. **Close:**
  `spring(response: 0.45, dampingFraction: 1.0)`. These replace
  `IslandView.reveal` (0.28 / 1.0) for the silhouette; `Motion.reposition` keeps
  0.28 / 1.0 for dots and bars.
- The window is sized to the **expanded** frame before the open spring starts
  and back to the compact frame after the close spring settles
  (`revealDuration` becomes 0.5 s, the settle time of the close spring). While
  closed, the window is the compact frame plus the inhale margin, so a cursor
  near the notch but outside the island never counts as a hover.
- **Content in:** the list is mounted when the spring starts, at `blur(20)` and
  opacity 0, and animates to `blur(0)`/opacity 1 over 0.25 s ease-out starting
  at 0.18 s (≈ 60 % of the open spring's travel); cards stagger 30 ms top to
  bottom. **Content out:** opacity 1 → 0 over 0.12 s, then the spring.
- **Reorder:** when a session moves in the list (e.g. starts waiting and goes to
  the top), only that card moves (`matchedGeometryEffect` keyed by session id);
  the others cross-fade.

### 5.3 Header (the 32 pt band)

- Left: the cat, at exactly its compact position (it does not move when the
  island opens).
- Right: the session dots (as in §4.2, dots form only) and a 22 pt "•••" button
  (white 10 % disc) that opens Settings.
- No title text. The 0.4 head tint (`LinearGradient` under the head) is removed
  — the rim carries the tone now.

### 5.4 Cards

List: 12 pt side margins, 6 pt between cards. Card: white 6 % fill (10 % on
hover), 12 pt radius (concentric with the 24 pt shell), padding 12/11/12/12, a
16 pt indicator column, then:

- **Head:** project name 15 semibold, flexible space, time 11 medium white 38 %,
  and `↗` 13 semibold white 80 % on hover when the session is routable.
- **Working:** task (13 regular, 62 %, one line), then the step line: step title
  12 medium 80 %, `2/5` 12 semibold tone, a 3 pt bar (track white 12 %). The
  indicator is the progress ring (§4.2) or a dot when there are no steps.
- **Waiting:** the reason line (§6.3) with inline code tokens (13 medium on
  white 10 %, radius 5), and an **Open ↗** pill in the tone with black text —
  the only coloured button in the island. The indicator is the dot with its
  halo.
- **Done:** the handoff summary (one line) and the handoff chips (unchanged
  behaviour: click opens, drag carries; restyled as white 10 % pills, 12
  semibold). Indicator: the check disc.
- **Crashed:** "the session ended unexpectedly" and a close `×` that removes the
  row (`SessionStore.dismiss(id:)`, new).
- **No route:** the card at 50 % opacity with the reason as an 11 pt line under
  it (always visible, not only on hover — a dimmed card with no explanation
  reads as broken).

Order is unchanged (`SessionStore.ordered`). **Idle sessions fold** into one
line at the end — dot, "2 open without a task", `›` — which expands in place to
show them as cards.

### 5.5 Footer — power

Shown only when there is something to say, 11 medium, white 50 %:

- a power glyph + "Mac stays awake — 2 agents working" while the assertion is
  held (`PowerManager.isHolding`, published through `AppState.isHoldingAwake`,
  new);
- "Mac can sleep in 1 min" during the grace period (`PowerManager` exposes
  `pendingReleaseAt`, read-only);
- right-aligned "Closed lid: on" when lid mode is on.

### 5.6 Empty and first run

- No sessions, hooks installed: "Agents are asleep" (15 semibold) and "Start
  `claude` in a terminal and the cat wakes up." No footer, no rim.
- Hooks not installed: one card — "Connect Claude Code", the one-sentence
  explanation, **Connect…** (white pill, black text; runs the existing confirm
  flow) and **Not now** (hides the card until next launch).

## 6. Peek

The island opens itself for a moment when an agent needs you.

### 6.1 When

| Event | Peek | Hold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| a session enters `waitingForYou(.permission)` or `(.question)` | yes | 3 s |
| a session enters `crashed` | yes | 3 s |
| a session enters `done` | yes, soft (setting, default on) | 1.5 s |
| `waitingForYou(.input)` (Claude's "waiting for your input" nudge after a finished turn) | no — the done peek already happened | — |
| `waitingForYou(.idle)` (the hook-less heuristic: a 5-minute transcript pause) | no — it is a guess | — |
| anything else (new session, step change, working) | never | — |

`Notification` messages containing "waiting for your input" map to a new
`WaitReason.input` (0.4 mapped them to `.question`). Not to `.idle`: `.idle` is
the hook-less guess and deliberately counts as *working* for the power policy
(`SessionStore.anyWorking`), which would keep the Mac awake after a finished
turn. `.input` counts as not working, like `.question`. The aggregate is
unaffected: every reason is waiting.

Each kind has a switch in Settings › Alerts (§8), all on by default.

### 6.2 Shape and behaviour

- Body 420 × 78 pt, fillet 14, bottom 24; opens and closes on the §5.2 springs.
  The compact band stays: cat and dots do not move.
- Line at y 42: project name (13 semibold) + reason (13 regular, 62 %, with
  code tokens), and on the right a pill: **Open ↗** (tone, black text) for
  waiting, **Open ↗** (white 10 %) for crashed, the first handoff chip for done.
- A 1.5 pt hairline in the tone along the bottom shrinks over the hold time —
  the time left.
- Hover holds the peek; leaving resumes the countdown from where it stopped.
  Clicking the line or the pill jumps to the session; hovering for the hover
  delay turns the peek into the expanded island (the peek is its first card's
  preview, so no content jumps).
- **Queue:** one peek at a time, 0.4 s apart. Events for different sessions
  within 1 s merge into "2 agents need you" + the project names, with **Show**
  (opens the expanded island).
- **Full screen:** the island hides while the notch screen shows a full-screen
  app (setting, default on) — but a waiting or crashed peek still appears.
  Detection, re-evaluated on `NSWorkspace.activeSpaceDidChangeNotification` and
  on app activation: the notch screen's menu bar is hidden
  (`NSMenu.menuBarVisible()` is false while the frontmost app is on that
  screen) and a layer-0 window of the frontmost app has bounds equal to the
  screen's frame (`CGWindowListCopyWindowInfo`, on-screen only). If this proves
  unreliable in capture tests, the switch ships disabled and the island simply
  stays, as in 0.4.
- **Screen locked:** no peeks. On unlock, if anything happened, one summary
  peek: "While you were away" + "2 done · 1 waiting", **Show**. This replaces
  the "While you were away" section in the list; `AwayLog` records kinds and
  project names (`AwayEntry.kind`, `.project`, new) instead of free text.
- **Sound** (if on) plays when the peek opens: `Purr` for waiting and crashed,
  `Glass` for done. No `NSUserNotification`/`UNUserNotification` ever.

### 6.3 The reason line

The Notification hook only says "Claude needs your permission to use Bash". The
transcript says what: the parser records the session's last `tool_use` as a
`PendingAction` (new, on `TranscriptActivity` and `Session`):

| Tool | Reason line |
| --- | --- |
| `Bash` | wants to run `‹first line of input.command, ≤ 40 chars, … if cut›` |
| `Edit`, `Write`, `MultiEdit`, `NotebookEdit` | wants to edit `‹file name›` |
| `WebFetch` | wants to open `‹host›` |
| any other tool | wants to use `‹tool name›` |
| `.question` wait | has a question for you |
| no pending action yet | the hook's own message, first sentence |

The action is taken from the last assistant `tool_use` that has no matching
`tool_result` yet, main session only (`isSidechain` false), and cleared on the
next `tool_result`, `UserPromptSubmit` or `Stop`. When the hook beats the
transcript, the line starts with the hook's message and updates in place when
the action arrives (cross-fade, 0.2 s).

## 7. Tokens (`ToneColor`, `MenuStyle.island`)

The island is always black, so its colours are fixed values, not system
colours: tones are the dark-mode system values — working `#30D158`, waiting
`#FF9F0A`, done `#0A84FF`, problem `#FF453A`, sleeping white 35 %. `ToneColor`
returns these explicitly on the island; the floating panel keeps semantic
colours where it sits on material. Whites: primary 100 %, secondary 62 %,
tertiary 38 %, card 6 %, card hover 10 %, hairline 8 %. Type: SF Pro, 15
semibold (names), 13 regular (body), 12 semibold (numbers, pills), 11 medium
(meta).

## 8. Settings window

A regular `NSWindow` (titled, closable, 720 × 520, not resizable) hosting a
SwiftUI `NavigationSplitView` with a 196 pt sidebar; opened by "•••", the
status-bar menu's **Settings… ⌘,**, and ⌘, while it is key. Opening it
activates the app (`NSApp.activate`), which is fine: the user asked for a
window. One instance; reopening brings it forward.

Panes and where each 0.4 switch goes (keys unchanged unless marked **new**):

- **General** — Show CodeCat as: Island / Floating cat / Menu bar only
  (`mascotDisplayMode` + `showMascot`; "Menu bar only" = `showMascot` off);

  Keep the Mac awake while agents work (`keepAwake`); "Stop below 15 %
  battery" (read-only); Keep agents running with the lid closed (`lidMode`,
  existing confirm + admin flow).
- **Cat** — a stage showing the chosen skin in all five states on black, the
  skin grid (four columns, white 2 pt border on the selected one, previews

- **Claude Code** — status ("Connected · 5 hooks · last event 4 s ago", or

## 13. Architecture notes

- `IslandController` keeps one `IslandPresentation` state instead of
  `menuLevel`: `.compact`, `.inhaled`, `.peek(PeekItem)`, `.expanded`. The
  transitions and timers (dwell, close delay, peek hold and queue) live in a
  small pure type in `CodeCatCore` — `IslandPresenter` — driven by events
  (`pointerEntered`, `pointerLeft`, `dwellElapsed`, `peekRequested`, `tick`),
  so the state machine is unit-tested without AppKit. The controller only
  executes its outputs (resize window, animate).
- `PeekScheduler` (`CodeCatCore`, new): turns session transitions into
  `PeekItem`s — rules of §6.1, merge within 1 s, queue, lock/unlock summary.
  Pure, tested.
- `PendingAction` extraction lives in `TranscriptParser`; reason text in a pure
  `PeekReason.text(for:)`.
- Shapes: `IslandLayout.rimPath` and a size-parameterised `silhouettePath`
  (both pure `CGPath`, tested for bounds and endpoints).
- Views: `IslandView` (compact + rim), `IslandExpandedView` (replaces
  `IslandMenuView`), `SessionCardView` (replaces the island style of
  `sessionRow`; the floating panel uses it too), `PeekView`,
  `SettingsWindowController` + `Settings/*Pane.swift`.
